### Key differences between heat stroke and heat exhaustion

- With **heat stroke&#32;**you get mental status changes. This includes confusion, slurred speech, delirium, agitation, seizures, or loss of consciousness. High Body Temp This is a medical emergency and requires immediate admission to hospital to prevent organ damage.
- Heat stroke occurs when the body's temperature regulation fails, pushing the core body temperature above 40° C (104° F).
- Heat exhaustion is a serious heat-related illness that occurs when your body overheats and loses excessive water and salt through heavy sweating. Symptoms typically include heavy sweating, pale or clammy skin, dizziness, nausea, headache, and a rapid pulse.
- Heat exhaustion can be treated by removing oneself from heat condition, removing excess clothing, sipping cold water or electrolyte drinks and applying cold compresses
